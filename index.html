How to Play

Each challenge puts you in a chat with a cautious assistant bot who is guarding a secret code. Your goal is to convince the bot to reveal it, using real manipulation and social-engineering techniques — the same ones scammers and manipulators use on people. Practicing them here, safely, helps you recognize them in real life.
